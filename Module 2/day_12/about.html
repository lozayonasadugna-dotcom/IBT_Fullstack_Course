My Biography

I am a Medical Radiology Technology graduate with a passion for Full-Stack Software Development and artificial intelligence.

Ever since childhood, I have loved troubleshooting devices and exploring technology. As the first-born child in my family, I developed strong leadership, initiative, and responsibility early on. Today, I am bridging my background in healthcare with software engineering to build impactful digital solutions.
