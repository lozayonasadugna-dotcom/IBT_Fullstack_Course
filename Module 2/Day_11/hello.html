Mastering diagnostic imaging techniques requires high precision and continuous clinical practice!

Diagnostic Modalities

  • Digital Radiography (X-Ray)
  • Computed Tomography (CT Scan)
  • Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI)
  • Diagnostic Ultrasound

Standard Patient Exam Protocol

  1. Verify patient identification and clinical request orders.
  2. Perform safety screening and equipment check.
  3. Position the patient correctly and acquire image sequence.
  4. Verify image quality and send scan to PACS for reporting.
