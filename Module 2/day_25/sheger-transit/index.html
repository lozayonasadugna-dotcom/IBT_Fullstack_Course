About Us

About Us

Sheger Transit is a digital mobility platform built to simplify daily commutes across Addis Ababa. We combine live transit mapping, smart route planning, and integrated digital ticketing into a single, intuitive hub tailored specifically for the modern commuter.

Our Vision

To create a seamlessly connected, fully cashless public transportation network where every passenger can navigate the city with absolute confidence, zero friction, and zero guesswork.

Our Mission

To empower commuters by providing real-time bus schedules, smart routing, and direct digital pass booking through local platforms like TeleBirr, transforming daily transit into a highly reliable and stress-free journey.

SHEGER TRANSIT – TERMS AND CONDITIONS

Last Updated: August 30, 2026

1. General Provisions

This document (the "Terms") constitutes a contractual agreement between you, the passenger ("you" or "Passenger"), and Sheger Transit ("we," "us," "our," or the "Company").

2. Consumer Rights

Under Ethiopian law, particularly the Trade Competition and Consumer Protection Proclamation No. 813/2013, consumers have the right to:

  • Receive clear and transparent information regarding contract terms and cancellation policies;
  • Request repair, re-service, or a refund if the service provided is defective or not rendered as agreed;
  • Seek compensation from the Federal Trade Competition and Consumer Protection Authority.

3. Passenger Cancellation and Refund Conditions

3.1 Cancellation Initiated by the Passenger

3.1.1 More Than 24 Hours Before Departure: Full refund of ticket price.

3.1.2 Between 2 and 24 Hours Before Departure: 50% refund of ticket price.

3.1.3 Less Than 2 Hours Before Departure: No refund will be issued.

3.1.4 No-Show: Failure to arrive at departure point without prior cancellation results in no refund.

3.2 Cancellation or Change Initiated by Sheger Transit

3.2.1 Company-Initiated Cancellation: Entitled to a full refund or free rebooking.

3.2.2 Significant Schedule Changes: Delays exceeding 2 hours or date changes entitle passenger to full refund.

3.3 Force Majeure

In unforeseen circumstances beyond reasonable control, Sheger Transit reserves sole discretion on refund/rebooking decisions.

4. Non-Refundable Circumstances

  • Ticket already used or partially used
  • Cancellation made less than 2 hours before departure
  • Passenger no-show
  • Ticket purchased >30 days ago and unused without refund request
  • Lost or stolen tickets
  • Denied boarding due to safety policy violations
  • Tickets bought via unauthorized third-party vendors

5. Refund Procedure and Processing Time

5.1 How to Apply: Online via official website, In-Person at customer service center, or via Customer Support hotline/email.

5.2 Required Information: Full name, contact info, ticket/order ID, trip details, and cancellation reason.

5.3 Processing Time: Complete applications processed within 10 business days.

6. Rebooking (Change of Schedule) Policy

  • >24 hours before departure: 1 free rebooking
  • 2–24 hours before departure: 20% rebooking fee
  • <2 hours before departure: Rebooking not permitted

7. Service Defects and Complaints

Failure to provide agreed service entitles passengers to full refund or free rebooking. Quality complaints are answered within 15 business days.

8. Limitation of Liability

Total liability is strictly limited to the actual fare paid.

9. Dispute Resolution

Disputes are addressed within 15 business days. Escalation is available via the Federal Trade Competition and Consumer Protection Authority.

10. Policy Amendments

Amendments take effect immediately upon website posting.
